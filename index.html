The choice

The first door

An internship was my way into the maritime industry. It later brought me to Shanghai, which is where the rest of this map happens.

A year and a half · two countries

Bureau Veritas

Business analytics, market reporting and fleet analysis. What I learned is that shipping runs on documents, on data nobody has cleaned, and on people willing to sign their name to a judgement.

The language

I passed an exam that some students fail after three years of study. It is the asset in this whole map that is hardest to copy.

The thing I built

Orange Chinese

A study app my friends needed because they were juggling five apps for one lesson. No sales yet. Five months of ten hour days taught me the difference between an idea and a product.
