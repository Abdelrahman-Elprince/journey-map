I did not plan my way into shipping. I spent a few years trying to become useful, and then a door opened earlier than I expected. This is the order it happened in.

The first prompt

It was bad. It was still better than nothing. That gap, between what a tool cannot do and what it almost can, is the thing I have been watching close ever since.

The choice

Business informatics

Everyone around me picked a side, code or commerce. I picked the seam between them, because a narrow shelf scared me more than hard work did.

3
The first door

Doha

An internship in an industry I had never touched, doing work nobody would call glamorous. It is the only reason I was in Shanghai a year later.

4
A year and a half · two countries

Bureau Veritas

Market reports nobody read closely and fleet data nobody had cleaned. The lesson took a while to land: in shipping the paperwork is the business, not the paperwork about the business.

5

An exam some students fail after three years. Three languages now, one of them learned against a clock I set for myself.

6
The thing I built

Orange Chinese

My friends stopped switching between five apps. I still have not sold a single subscription. Building it was the easy half, and I only learned that by finishing it.

7
Now

Fudan

International Business, School of Economics. A credential buys a seat at the table. It does not buy the conversation, which is the part I am working on.

8
Next

The chartering desk

Where a voyage becomes a price and a price becomes a promise. The model can do the arithmetic. I want to be the one who can be held to the promise.

In all honesty, we like to believe we are steering our own lives. Most of the time we are only choosing how we meet what arrives. I will do my best with what I have been given, and let the universe decide the rest.
